# About Kohinoor Tez

Kohinoor Tez is the storefront of Adwani Publishing House, an educational
publisher based in Nagpur, Maharashtra. We have published study guides for
Maharashtra students since 1998.

We publish over 325 titles across Classes Nursery to 12, covering the
Maharashtra State Board and CBSE curricula, in Marathi medium, Semi-English
medium and English medium. We supply more than 1,400 schools and retailers
across Maharashtra.

## Our book series

**Kohinoor** is the flagship series — complete chapter-wise guides covering
every core subject from Class 5 through Class 12. Available in Marathi,
Semi-English and English medium, for both State Board and CBSE.

**Spark** is written for the Science stream in Classes 11 and 12, English
medium only. It covers Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Mathematics with solved
board papers.

**Vidyamitra** serves the middle years, Classes 5 to 8, in Marathi and
Semi-English medium. It focuses on practice and reinforcement.

**WinWings** is exam-focused. It produces combo sets and guessing papers for
Classes 9 and 10, aimed at SSC board preparation.

**Ekatmik** publishes integrated, curriculum-aligned workbooks, including our
Nursery and pre-primary titles.

## What is in a Kohinoor guide

Every guide contains chapter-wise summarised theory, all textbook exercises
solved, board-pattern practice questions, previous years' questions marked by
frequency, and full-length practice papers in the board-exam titles.

Guides are written by teachers who teach the same syllabus in Maharashtra
classrooms. Every edition states its edition year on the product page, and we
reprint whenever the State Board or CBSE revises a syllabus.
