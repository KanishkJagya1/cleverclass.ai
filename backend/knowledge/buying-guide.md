# Choosing the right books

## Choosing a medium

Marathi medium means every subject is taught and printed in Marathi.

Semi-English medium means Mathematics and Science are in English while the
remaining subjects are in Marathi. This is the most common choice in
Maharashtra State Board schools today.

English medium means every subject is in English.

Buy the medium your school teaches in, not the one you are most comfortable
reading. Exam answers must be written in the school's medium.

Every product page has a medium switch, so you can move between the Marathi,
Semi-English and English editions of the same title without losing your place.

## Choosing between individual books and a combo pack

A combo pack contains every core subject guide for one class, in one medium,
shipped together. Combo packs cost 15% to 30% less than buying the same titles
separately, and most of them clear the ₹200 free-shipping threshold on their
own.

Buy a combo pack if you need a full year of books. Buy individual titles if
your child needs help in one or two subjects only.

## Classes 11 and 12

Junior college sets differ by stream, so the right pack depends on the subject
combination your college follows.

Science PCM covers Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.
Science PCB covers Physics, Chemistry and Biology.
Science PCBM covers Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Mathematics.
Commerce covers Book Keeping and Accountancy, Economics, Organisation of
Commerce and Mathematics.
Arts covers History, Political Science, Geography and Sociology.

For Class 12 Science in English medium, the Spark series is the usual choice.
For Marathi and Semi-English medium, use Kohinoor.

## Class 10 and board-exam preparation

For SSC preparation, the Kohinoor Class 10 guides cover the full syllabus.
WinWings additionally publishes guessing papers and question banks aimed
specifically at the board exam. Many families buy both.

## Key Notes

Key Notes are concise chapter-wise summaries for Classes 5 to 10, available in
Marathi and Semi-English medium. The preview is free and needs no sign-up or
email address.

Key Notes summarise the syllabus. The full guide adds solved textbook
exercises, board-pattern questions, marked previous-year questions and
practice papers. Students revising a chapter they already understand can use
the notes alone; students preparing to answer an exam paper need the guide.
