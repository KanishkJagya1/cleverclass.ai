# Shipping, returns and ordering

## Shipping charges

Shipping is free on all orders of ₹200 and above. On orders below ₹200 a flat
₹40 shipping charge applies, anywhere in India. Most combo packs are priced
above ₹200, so they ship free on their own.

## Delivery time

Orders delivered within Maharashtra usually arrive in 3 to 5 working days.
Orders to the rest of India usually take 5 to 8 working days. Remote pincodes
can take longer. Orders placed on a Sunday or public holiday are dispatched on
the next working day. All parcels ship from our Nagpur office.

You receive a tracking reference by SMS and email once the parcel leaves us.

## Returns

Unused books in original condition can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
Books that have been written in, highlighted or damaged cannot be returned.

If we sent the wrong title, the wrong medium or a damaged copy, we cover the
return shipping and send the correct book at no extra cost. Tell us within 48
hours of delivery.

If you changed your mind, return shipping is at your cost. Once the book
reaches us in resaleable condition we refund the item price.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days
of the return being received and checked.

To start a return, email kohinoortezz@gmail.com or call +91 71042 99010 with
your order reference.
